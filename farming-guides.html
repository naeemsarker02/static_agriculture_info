  1. Collect a mix of green waste, such as vegetable scraps, and brown waste, such as dry leaves.
  2. Layer greens and browns in roughly equal parts in a compost bin or pile.
  3. Turn the pile every one to two weeks to aerate and speed up decomposition.
  4. Keep the pile as moist as a wrung-out sponge, watering if it dries out.
  5. Let the compost mature for two to three months until it becomes dark and crumbly.
  6. Mix finished compost into topsoil before planting to boost fertility.

Tip: Avoid adding meat, dairy, or oily foods to the pile as they attract pests and slow composting.
